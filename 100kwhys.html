The Big Book of 100,000 Whys for Intelligent Men

A Complete Encyclopedia of Answers to Feed your Curiosity about the World.

Antonio Tropiano

You’ve been asking “why” your whole life. This book is what happens when someone finally takes those questions seriously.

Why do nations fight over invisible lines on a map?
Why do markets panic over tiny interest rate changes?
Why does one man become obsessed with the gym and another with money, power, or status?

The Big Book of 100,000 Whys for Intelligent Men is a sharp, entertaining guide for men who don’t want fluff, clichés, or shallow self‑help. It takes 300+ questions you actually think about, power, risk, work, women, status, money, meaning, and explains the deeper forces behind them in clear language you can use.

Each question is answered in a few focused paragraphs: clear enough to read at night when you’re tired, deep enough to respect your intelligence, and practical enough that you can’t unsee the logic once you’ve read it.
